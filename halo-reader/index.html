Halo Reader

An ebook reader for EPUB, FB2, PDF and comics that keeps your books, your place, highlights and notes in step on every phone and tablet, through a cloud you already own.

  • Reads what you have. EPUB, FB2 and FB2.ZIP in any encoding, MOBI and AZW3, PDF and DjVu, CBZ and CBR comics, Word (DOCX), RTF, web pages, Markdown and plain text.
  • Made for long reading. Book-quality fonts, six page colours from white to black, page turns or scrolling, and pages or time left in the chapter.
  • Highlights and notes. Five colours, notes, bookmarks, search in the book, offline dictionaries, and export of your highlights.
  • Sync without an account. Connect Google Drive or your own WebDAV server and pick up on another device where you stopped.
  • Read aloud. Listen to any book with the voices on your device, with the screen off.
  • Private shelf. Books behind a code, password or Face ID, kept encrypted and erased after too many wrong tries.
  • Free book catalogs. Browse and download from Project Gutenberg or your own calibre server.
  • Private by design. No ads, no analytics, no sign-up. Your library is never sent to us.
