Tailtick privacy policy

Effective date: 6 November 2026

In short: your pets, reminders, care history, weights, notes and photos are stored only on your phone. There is no account, no sign-in and no advertising. The app sends two kinds of technical data to Google Firebase: crash reports (on unless you turn them off) and anonymous usage statistics (only if you say yes to "Help improve Tailtick"). Neither ever includes your pets' names, notes, photos, dates or weights.

What stays on your phone

  • Pets and their details: name, species, breed, birthday, sex, microchip number, vet's name and phone, notes and photo.
  • Care: reminders, schedules, doses left in a pack, the history of what was done or skipped, weights, symptoms and vet visits.
  • Settings: reminder times, weight unit, and your privacy choices.

We never receive, see or store any of this. We have no servers that hold your records. Reminders are scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to set them up.

What is sent, and why

1. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics, by Google)

If the app crashes or hits a serious error, it sends a crash report so we can find and fix the bug. A report contains the error and which code failed, the app version, device model, operating system version and similar technical details, and a random installation identifier used to count how many installations a crash affects (it isn't your advertising ID or a hardware ID, and it changes if you reinstall).

A report never contains your pets' details, notes, photos or care records. We don't attach names, email addresses or user IDs.

Your choice: turn crash reports off at any time in Settings › Privacy › Crash reports.

Retention: Firebase keeps crash data for 90 days and then deletes it.

2. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), only with your yes

If you answer Yes, share to "Help improve Tailtick" when you set the app up, or turn on Settings › Privacy › Usage statistics, the app sends anonymous events that tell us which features are used, for example "a pet was added", "a reminder was created", "a dose was marked done", "the vet summary was made", or "the Pro screen was shown". Events carry no names, titles, notes, dates, weights or photos. Until you have answered, nothing is sent; events wait in the phone's memory and are dropped if you say no.

Advertising features are switched off: no advertising ID, no ad personalisation, no ad user data, and nothing is shared with advertisers. Turn usage statistics off at any time in the same place. Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months.

3. Purchases

Tailtick Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Google Play or the App Store. We don't receive your payment details; the store tells the app only whether Pro is owned.

4. Things you choose to send

When you share a vet summary (PDF) or save a backup file, it goes only to the app or place you pick, and that app's privacy policy then applies. Tailtick doesn't send a copy anywhere else.

5. Your calendar, if you turn it on

If you turn on Show in your calendar, Tailtick writes your care dates (what is due, for which pet, when, and any dose you entered) into the calendar on your phone that you pick, and keeps them up to date. That calendar may be synced by its provider (for example Google or Apple) under your account, and anyone you share it with can see those events. Tailtick only changes or removes the events it added, and removes them all when you turn it off. Add to calendar on a single reminder opens your phone's own new event screen, and nothing is saved until you save it there.

Backups

On Android, your phone may include Tailtick's data in its own backup to your Google account, depending on your device settings; on iPhone, iCloud or computer backups of the device include it. These backups are made by Google or Apple under your account, and we can't read them. Tailtick also lets you save a backup file yourself, which stays wherever you put it.

What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising or advertising SDKs, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera or microphone. Photos: only the one you pick in the system photo picker.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show your care reminders and an optional Sunday summary.
  • Alarms and reminders (Android, optional): so reminders arrive on the minute even in battery saver.
  • Photos: only the photo you pick for a pet, through the system picker.
  • Calendar (optional): asked only when you turn on Show in your calendar, to list your calendars and write the care dates to the one you pick.
  • Internet: used only for crash reports, usage statistics if you allow them, and the store purchase check.

Service providers

Crash reports and usage statistics are processed by Google LLC (Firebase) on our behalf, encrypted in transit (Firebase privacy information). We have no other processors.

Data retention and deletion

Your records stay on your phone until you delete them in the app or uninstall it, which removes all app data. To ask us to delete crash or statistics data from your installation sooner, email haloapps.dev@gmail.com. Because we hold no names or emails, tell us roughly when you used the app and your device model.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data. The only personal data we could hold is the technical data above. Our legal basis is our legitimate interest in keeping the app working for crash reports (you can opt out at any time) and your consent for usage statistics (you can withdraw it at any time).

Not veterinary advice

Tailtick keeps your records and reminds you. It isn't veterinary advice: always follow your vet and the product label.

Children

Tailtick is suitable for all ages and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information beyond the technical data described above, from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what is sent, we will update this policy before release. Any new network feature will be clearly labelled.
