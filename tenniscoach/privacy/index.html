Tennis AI Coach privacy policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · Published by Halo Apps · Contact: haloapps.dev@gmail.com

In short: the camera picture is analysed on your phone, and your videos, scores and stats are stored only on your phone. They leave it only when you save or share a highlight clip yourself. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting SDKs, and it never uses the microphone. Its only network use is downloading ball-model updates, which you can turn off, together with a Google Play Integrity check before a download.

1. What stays on your phone

  • Camera picture. While a session runs, the app reads the camera picture to find the court and follow the ball. This happens on your phone; frames are not stored or sent anywhere.
  • Session videos. If Record video is on (it is by default, so you can save highlights), the session is recorded into the app's private storage on your phone. No sound is recorded.
  • Sessions. Player names you type, the score, each point, the shots the tracker measured (time, speed, where the ball bounced) and your settings.
  • Highlight clips. When you tap Save clip, a short clip is saved to your phone's Movies folder (Movies / Tennis AI Coach). From there it goes only where you share it.

We never receive, see or store any of this. We have no servers that hold your videos or sessions, and the app opts out of automatic cloud backups of its data.

2. What is sent, and why

Ball-model updates

The tracker's ball model can be improved without a new app version. At most once a day, and when you tap Check now in Settings, the app downloads a small signed list of model packages from our website (haloapps-dev.github.io, hosted by GitHub Pages) and, if there is a newer package, downloads it from the same site. These requests carry no information about you or your sessions. Like any website, GitHub's servers receive your IP address and basic request details to deliver the files (see GitHub's privacy policies). We do not log or receive them.

Google Play Integrity check

Before a model package is downloaded, the app asks Google Play to confirm that it is the genuine app installed from Google Play on a genuine device. Google Play processes information about the app, the device and your Play account's licence for this app to give its answer (Play Integrity API; Google privacy policy). The app receives only a sealed answer and uses it to decide whether to download the package. It is not used for advertising or tracking.

Your choice: turn Download model updates off in Settings and the app makes neither request; it then uses the model built into the app.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: to film the court and follow the ball.
  • Internet: only for the model updates and the check above.
  • The app does not ask for the microphone, location, contacts or your photo library. Saving a clip to Movies needs no extra permission on Android 10 and later.

4. What we do not do

No accounts or sign-in. No ads or advertising IDs. No analytics or usage statistics. No crash reporting. No selling or sharing of data. No face recognition or identification of players: the tracker follows the ball and the court lines only.

5. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything stays on your phone until you delete it. Deleting a session in the app deletes its score, stats and video. Uninstalling the app deletes all of its data; highlight clips you saved to Movies stay there until you delete them.

6. Legal bases and your rights

We process no personal data on our own servers. Under the UK and EU GDPR, the model update check relies on our legitimate interest in delivering working, untampered models, and you can turn it off at any time. You have rights to access, delete or object to the processing of personal data; because we hold none, there is nothing for us to return or delete, but you can contact us with any question.

7. Children

Tennis AI Coach is a general-audience sports app and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone. If you film other people, including children, ask their (or their parent's) permission first.

8. Security

Model packages are signed by Halo Apps and checked against their signature and checksums before use; downloads use HTTPS. Your data stays in the app's private storage on your phone.

9. Changes

If a future version changes what is sent, we will update this policy before that version is released. Any new network feature will be clearly labelled in the app.
