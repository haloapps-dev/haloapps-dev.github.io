Tennis AI Coach

Clip your phone behind the baseline and play. The app follows the ball, suggests each point for a one-tap confirm, measures shot speed and says how sure it is, and saves highlights.

  • Score assist. Points are suggested from the ball's bounces; one tap confirms or changes them, and big buttons score by hand at any time.
  • Shot speed with confidence. Every speed comes with its spread, and uncertain ones are marked as such.
  • Works offline. Tracking runs on the phone. Courts with no signal are fine.
  • Private by default. Video and sessions stay on your phone. No account, no ads, no analytics.
