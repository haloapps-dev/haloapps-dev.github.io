How to delete duplicate photos on Android

Duplicates sneak in from chat apps, re-downloads, edits saved as copies and photos restored from a backup. Android has no single "duplicates" button that works on every phone, but there are a few reliable ways to find them. Here they are, from the quickest to the most thorough.

Steps: delete exact duplicates with Files by Google

  1. Open Files by Google.
  2. Tap Menu, then Clean.
  3. On the Duplicate files card, tap Select files.
  4. Pick the copies to delete. The original is marked with an Original badge; keep that one.
  5. Tap Move to Bin at the bottom and confirm.

If you do not see the card, Files has not found any exact duplicates. Source: Files by Google Help: Delete duplicate files.

Steps: delete duplicates in Samsung Gallery

  1. Open Gallery and tap Menu (three lines).
  2. Tap Clean out, then Delete duplicate pictures. The option may not appear if there are no duplicates.
  3. Tap Delete duplicates. Gallery removes the copies and keeps the originals.

Samsung Gallery can also stack similar-looking pictures into one thumbnail with a small number in the corner. Use More options > Ungroup similar images if you want to see every shot. Menu names vary by One UI version. Source: Samsung: Use the Gallery app.

What about Google Photos?

Google Photos has no button for finding duplicates on your phone, but many people clean up in it anyway. Two things to know before you delete there:

  • Deleting in the Google Photos app removes the item from your phone and from your backup, on every device where backup is on. To remove only the phone copy, use Delete from device.
  • Deleted items go to Trash and stay there for 30 days (Google Photos Help).

Tips for keeping the right copy

  • When two copies differ in size, keep the bigger one. The smaller one is often a compressed version from a chat app.
  • If you edited a photo and saved a copy, decide whether you want the edit, the original, or both, before you clear the pair.
  • Photos saved from WhatsApp and other messengers usually live in their own folders, so the same picture can appear twice in your gallery.

Empty the bin to get the space back

Files, Gallery and Google Photos all move deleted items to a bin or trash first, and they keep using space until it is emptied. In Samsung Gallery: Menu > Trash > More options > Empty. Check nothing you need is there, then empty it. For more ways to clear space, see how to free up storage on Android by cleaning up photos and videos.

Menu names differ between phone makers, app versions and Android versions. If a menu is missing, update the app or search for it in the app's settings.
