Samsung Gallery storage cleanup: free up space on a Galaxy phone

On most Galaxy phones, photos and videos are the biggest thing in storage. Samsung already gives you three places to clean them up: the Gallery app, the My Files app and the Storage screen in Settings. Each one finds something different, and deleted items keep using space until the Recycle bin is emptied. Here is where each tool lives, what it removes, and a safe order to use them in.

1. See how much space Gallery really uses

  1. Open Settings and tap Battery and device care (or Device care).
  2. Tap Storage. You will see how much space is free and how much is used by images, videos, apps and other files.
  3. If images and videos are the biggest part, the steps below will help most. If apps are, start with Unused apps on the same screen.

Source: Samsung UK: How to clear or free up internal storage on your phone.

2. Delete duplicate pictures in Gallery

Saved copies from chats, re-downloads and edits saved twice are exact duplicates. Gallery can find them for you:

  1. Open Gallery and tap Menu (three horizontal lines).
  2. Under Clean out, tap Delete duplicate pictures.
  3. Check the list, tap Delete duplicates, then Delete and Move to Trash (or Recycle bin) to confirm. Samsung says this removes the duplicates and keeps the originals.

Source: Samsung US: Use the Gallery app on your Galaxy phone or tablet. More detail: how to delete duplicate photos on Android.

3. Clear similar shots and bursts

Bursts and near-identical shots are not exact copies, so the duplicate tool skips them. On newer Galaxy phones, Gallery groups similar-looking pictures under one thumbnail with a small number (2, 3 and so on) in the corner.

  1. In the Pictures tab, tap a thumbnail with a number to see every shot in the group.
  2. Pick the one you want to keep. Touch and hold the others, tap Delete, then Move to Trash.
  3. If you prefer to see every picture separately, tap More options (three dots) > Ungroup similar images.

4. Find large videos and files

A handful of long videos can use more space than thousands of photos. Samsung has two ways to list big files:

  • Settings: Battery and device care > Storage > Large files. Select what you don't need and tap Delete.
  • My Files: open My Files and tap Analyse storage. It shows duplicate files, large files and unused files from all folders, not just the Gallery (Samsung UK: How to use Samsung My Files).

Play a video before you delete it, and remember that large files can include things outside your gallery, such as downloaded installers. Full steps: how to find large videos taking up space on Android.

5. Use Duplicate files carefully

The Duplicate files lists in Settings > Storage and in My Files > Analyse storage cover all file types. Samsung warns that if you select every copy in a group, the original is deleted too. Always leave one file in each group unselected (Samsung: How to find and remove duplicate files on your Galaxy device).

6. Empty the Recycle bin to get the space back

Deleting from Gallery moves items to its Recycle bin, where Samsung says they stay for 30 days. Until then they still use storage.

  1. Open Gallery, tap Menu and then Recycle bin (or Trash).
  2. Look through it once more. To bring something back, tap Edit, select it and tap Restore.
  3. To free the space, tap More options (three dots) > Empty, then Delete.

You can also empty bins from Settings > Battery and device care > Storage: under Recycle bin, tap an app and tap Empty. Emptying cannot be undone. Sources: Samsung UK: How to recover deleted photos and the Gallery guide above. More on bins: how the Trash and Recycle bin work on Android.

Common questions

Why is my Samsung storage still full after deleting photos?

Deleted photos sit in the Gallery Recycle bin for 30 days and keep using space. Empty the bin in Gallery or under Settings > Battery and device care > Storage.

Where is "Clean out" in Samsung Gallery?

Tap Menu (three lines) in Gallery. Clean out lists Delete duplicate pictures when Gallery finds duplicates; if you have none, the option may not appear.

Is the Gallery duplicate tool the same as Duplicate files in My Files?

No. The Gallery tool looks at pictures and keeps the originals for you. Duplicate files in My Files and Settings looks at all file types, and you choose which copy stays, so leave one in each group.
