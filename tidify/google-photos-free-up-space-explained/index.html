Google Photos "Free up space" explained: what it deletes and what stays

"Free up space on this device" sounds scary: will it delete your photos? In short, it removes the copies on your phone of photos and videos that are already backed up, and keeps them in your Google account. It does not make your gallery smaller in the cloud, and it is not the same as Delete. Here is exactly what happens.

What "Free up space" removes, and what stays

ItemAfter "Free up space"
Backed-up photos and videos on the phoneThe phone copy is deleted. The backed-up copy stays in your Google Photos library.
Photos and videos that are not backed upNot touched. Only items that are safely backed up are removed.
Photos and videos less than 30 days oldMay be kept on the phone, even though they are backed up.
Your Google Photos libraryUnchanged. You still see everything in the Google Photos app and at photos.google.com.
Your phone's own gallery appRemoved items no longer show there, because they are no longer on the phone.
Your Google account storage (15 GB shared)Unchanged. Freeing phone space does not free any cloud space.

Source: Free up space on your device, Google Photos Help. You also cannot see the removed items when you are offline or signed out of Google Photos.

How to use "Free up space" safely

  1. Check backup is complete. Open Google Photos and tap your profile photo or initial at the top right. It should say "Backup complete". If backup is off or still running, wait (Back up photos and videos).
  2. Check the right account. You can back up to only one Google account at a time. If you use more than one, make sure the account you are signed in to is the one holding your backup.
  3. Open the option. Tap your profile photo or initial, then Free up space on this device.
  4. Read the number. Google Photos shows how much space you will get back. Tap Free up [size] from device to remove those phone copies.
  5. Check a few photos. Open Google Photos or photos.google.com and look at some older photos to confirm they are still there.

Menu names can change between Google Photos versions, and some phone makers add their own gallery and cloud apps. The steps above are for the Google Photos app on Android.

"Free up space" is not "Delete"

This is the mistake that loses photos. In the Google Photos app:

  • Trash (Delete) removes the item from your phone and from your Google Photos backup, and from every other device with backup on. It goes to Trash for 30 days, then it is gone for good (Delete photos and videos, Google Photos Help).
  • Delete from device (open a photo, then More) removes only the phone copy of that one item, like "Free up space" does for many items at once.
  • Free up space on this device removes the phone copies of everything already backed up.

So if you want a photo gone everywhere, use Trash. If you want it off the phone but still in your account, use "Free up space" or "Delete from device".

What about Storage saver?

"Storage saver" is a backup quality, not a clean-up button. You find it in Google Photos under your profile photo, then Google Photos settings > Backup > Photos & videos quality (Choose the backup quality, Google Photos Help).

  • Original quality keeps files as you took them.
  • Storage saver compresses backups: photos larger than 16 MP are resized to 16 MP, and videos above 1080p are resized to 1080p. Some details, like closed captions, can be lost.
  • Both count toward your Google account storage. Only items backed up in High quality (the old name for Storage saver) before 1 June 2021 are free.
  • Some countries also have an Express option with much smaller files. If you cannot see it, it is not offered where you are.

The catch: if you back up in Storage saver and then use "Free up space", the full-quality original is removed from your phone and only the compressed copy is left. If some photos matter to you at full size, copy them to a computer or keep them on the phone first.

Common questions

Will "Free up space" delete photos from Google Photos?

No. It only removes the copies on the phone of items that are already backed up. They stay in your library.

Why did I not get as much space back as I expected?

Recent items (under 30 days old) may stay on the phone, items that are not backed up are skipped, and big chat media or downloads may live outside your photo folders. Check Settings > Storage to see what else is using space, and read how to free up storage on Android and how to clear WhatsApp media.

Does it free up my 15 GB of Google storage?

No. To free cloud storage you have to delete items from Google Photos itself (and empty Trash), or remove large files elsewhere in your Google account.

Can I get the phone copies back?

You can download a photo or video from Google Photos again whenever you need it. If you used Storage saver, the download is the compressed copy.

Keep fewer photos, not just fewer phone copies

"Free up space" moves the problem to your Google account, where the 15 GB fills up too. A few minutes of real clean-up saves space on both: start with duplicate photos and screenshots in bulk, then empty the Trash.
