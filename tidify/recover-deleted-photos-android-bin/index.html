How to recover deleted photos on Android from the Trash or Recycle bin

Deleted a photo by mistake? Don't panic. Most Android gallery apps don't delete straight away: they move the photo to a Trash or Recycle bin and keep it there for 30 days. First work out which app you deleted it in, then open that app's bin and tap Restore. Here is where to look.

Restore a photo in Google Photos

  1. Open the Google Photos app.
  2. At the bottom, tap Collections, then Trash.
  3. Touch and hold the photo or video you want back. You can tap more to select several.
  4. At the bottom, tap Restore.

The photo goes back to your phone's gallery app, your Google Photos library and any albums it was in. If you deleted a backed-up photo, it was also removed from your backup and every device signed in to that account, so restoring brings it back everywhere.

Restore a photo in Samsung Gallery

  1. Open the Gallery app.
  2. Tap Menu (three horizontal lines), then Recycle bin. On some models and software versions it is called Trash.
  3. Tap Edit (or touch and hold a photo) and select what you want back. Tap All to pick everything.
  4. Tap Restore (or Restore all).

Samsung keeps Gallery deletions for 30 days, and on One UI 6 and later the Recycle bin in My Files can't be turned off. If you can't find the menu, update your phone's software first: Samsung says screens can vary by carrier, model and software version.

Restore a file in Files by Google

  1. Open Files by Google.
  2. Tap Menu at the top left, then Trash.
  3. Select the files, tap Restore, then Restore again to confirm.

Files stay in this Trash for 30 days. Google says the feature started with Android 11 and is rolling out to other versions, so older phones may not have it.

I can't find the photo in any bin

  • Check the other apps. A photo deleted in Samsung Gallery is not in the Google Photos Trash, and the other way round. Look in every gallery and file app on the phone.
  • Check the right Google account. If you use more than one, switch accounts in Google Photos (tap your profile photo) and look in each Trash. You can also check at photos.google.com.
  • Was it deleted from the phone only? If you used "Free up space" or "Delete from device", the photo is still in your Google Photos library, not in the Trash. See what Google Photos "Free up space" really deletes.
  • Search for it. In Google Photos, try searching for a place, a person or a date.
  • Was it more than 30 days ago? Then the bin has emptied it. Google says permanently deleted items can't be restored. Check older backups you might have: a computer, a memory card, or a cloud service from your phone maker.

Be careful with "photo recovery" apps that promise to bring back permanently deleted photos. They can't undo a permanent delete from a cloud service, and many ask for wide access to your files.

Common questions

How long do deleted photos stay on Android?

In Google Photos, Samsung Gallery, Samsung My Files and Files by Google, 30 days. After that they are deleted for good.

Do photos in the Trash still use space?

Yes, until the bin is emptied or the 30 days are up. If you need the space now and you are sure, empty the bin: in Google Photos, open Collections > Trash, tap More, then Empty Trash.

Why does Google Photos say "Delete permanently" instead of "Move to Trash"?

Google says that prompt means your Trash is full. Empty some of it, or check carefully before you confirm.
