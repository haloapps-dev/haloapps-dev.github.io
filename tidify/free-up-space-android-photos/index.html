How to free up storage on Android by cleaning up photos and videos

"Storage full" usually means your gallery, not your apps. Videos, screenshots, near-identical shots and chat media pile up for years. Here is a calm order to clear them without losing anything you care about.

1. See what is actually using the space

Open Settings > Storage (on some phones Settings > Battery and device care > Storage). It shows how much goes to images, videos, apps and other files. If images and videos are the biggest block, start with the steps below.

2. Clear the biggest wins first

What to look forWhy it helps
Long and old videosOne minute of 4K video can take more space than hundreds of photos. Sort by size and start at the top.
ScreenshotsMost were useful for a day. Thousands of them add up. See how to delete screenshots on Android in bulk.
Duplicates and near-duplicatesBursts and five tries of the same shot. Keep the best one. See how to delete duplicate photos on Android.
Chat mediaWhatsApp and other messengers save every photo, video and meme you receive. In WhatsApp, go to Settings > Storage and data > Manage storage to see the largest chats.
DownloadsOld PDFs, APKs and saved images in the Downloads folder.

3. Empty the bin, or you get nothing back

Deleted photos usually go to a bin or trash first and still use space until it is emptied. In Google Photos, deleted items stay in Trash for 30 days (Google Photos Help). Your gallery app may have its own Recycle bin. Check the bin, then empty it to get the space back.

4. Use your backup, carefully

If you back up to Google Photos, its Free up space option removes the phone copies of photos and videos that are already backed up. They stay in your Google account. Check that backup is finished, and remember that "Storage saver" backups are not full quality.

5. Keep it from filling up again

  • Spend two minutes a week clearing new screenshots and duplicates, instead of an hour once a year.
  • Turn off auto-download of media in busy group chats.
  • Record video at 1080p unless you really need 4K.

Menu names differ between phone makers and Android versions. If you cannot find a menu, search for "Storage" in Settings.
