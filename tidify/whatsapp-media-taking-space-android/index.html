WhatsApp media taking up space on Android: how to clear it

Every photo, video, voice note and sticker sent to you in WhatsApp is saved on your phone by default, in busy group chats too. After a year or two that can be a large part of your storage. Here is how to see where the space goes, clear it safely, and stop it filling up again.

Where WhatsApp media hides on your phone

Steps: find and delete large WhatsApp media

  1. Open WhatsApp and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Storage and data, then Manage storage. The space WhatsApp uses on your phone is shown at the top.
  3. Start with the items that are large (larger than 5 MB) or have been forwarded many times. Forwarded videos and memes from groups are usually safe to lose.
  4. Below that, chats are listed by how much space their media uses. Open the biggest one; files are sorted from largest to smallest.
  5. Tap and hold an item to select it, tap more items (or Select all), then tap the delete icon and Delete. If WhatsApp offers Delete any copies, tick it to remove duplicate copies too.

You can also open a chat, tap its name and tap Manage storage to see just that chat. Deleting an item only removes it for you; others in the chat still have it. You may not be able to download it again from the chat. Source: WhatsApp Help Center: How to free up storage on WhatsApp.

Steps: clear all media from one chat

Useful for a busy group where you want the messages but not years of photos and videos.

  1. Open the chat.
  2. Tap More options (the three dots), then More > Clear chat.
  3. Select Clear media files. Tick or untick Videos and GIFs, Photos, Stickers, Documents and Audio. Leave Clear starred messages unticked to keep starred items.
  4. Tap Clear media.

Be careful not to tap the plain Clear chat option, which removes the messages as well. The chat itself stays in your list either way. Source: WhatsApp Help Center.

Steps: remove WhatsApp copies from your gallery

  • Files by Google: tap Menu > Clean. On the Delete WhatsApp media card, tap Select files, choose what to remove and tap Move to Trash. Source: Files by Google Help: Delete WhatsApp media files. The card only shows when Files finds WhatsApp media.
  • Gallery app: open Albums and look for WhatsApp Images and WhatsApp Video. Select what you do not need and delete it. In Google Photos, these are folders on your device under Collections; deleting there also removes backed-up copies, so read how to free up storage on Android first if backup is on.

Stop WhatsApp filling your phone again

  • Turn off Media visibility. In Settings > Chats, turn off Media visibility so new media is not saved to your gallery. For one noisy group only: open the group, tap its name, then Media visibility > No > OK. Source: WhatsApp Help Center: How to stop WhatsApp from saving media to your phone.
  • Limit auto-download. In Settings > Storage and data, under Media auto-download, set what downloads When using mobile data, When connected on Wi-Fi and When roaming. Untick Videos and you only download the ones you tap. Source: WhatsApp Help Center: How to configure auto-download.
  • Check in monthly. Open Manage storage once a month and clear the large and forwarded items. It takes a minute.

Empty the trash to get the space back

Files by Google and gallery apps move deleted items to a trash first, and they still use space until it is emptied. Check nothing you need is there, then empty it. Next, clear the other big space users: screenshots in bulk and duplicate photos.
