How to delete duplicate photos on iPhone

Good news first: since iOS 16, the Photos app looks for duplicates on its own and collects them in a Duplicates album. You do not need a separate app to clear exact copies. Here is where to find the album on your iOS version, what the Merge button actually does, and what to do when the album is not there.

Where the Duplicates album is

iOS versionPath in the Photos app
iOS 18 and laterCollections (bottom of the screen), scroll down to Utilities, then Duplicates
iOS 16 and iOS 17Albums, scroll down to Utilities, then Duplicates
iOS 15 and earlierNo Duplicates album. Update iOS if your iPhone supports it, or compare photos by eye.

If you do not see Duplicates under Utilities, your iPhone has not found any duplicates yet. See why the album may be missing below.

Steps: merge duplicate photos and videos

  1. Open Photos.
  2. Tap Collections (on iOS 16 and 17, Albums), scroll down and tap Duplicates under Utilities. Copies of the same photo or video appear side by side.
  3. Tap Merge next to one set, then tap Merge [number] Items to confirm.
  4. To clear many sets at once, tap Select, then Select All (or Select on each set you want), then Merge, and confirm.

Merging keeps one photo in your library, combining the highest quality version with the relevant data from the copies. The other copies move to the Recently Deleted album. Sources: iPhone User Guide: Merge duplicate photos and videos and Apple Support: Delete photos on your iPhone or iPad.

Why the Duplicates album may be missing

  • Your iPhone is still scanning. Photos finds duplicates in the background while the iPhone is locked and charging. With a big library this can take from a few minutes to a few days. To check, open Photos, tap Library and scroll to the bottom: a status message appears there while your library is being indexed.
  • There are no duplicates. The album only appears when there is something in it.
  • Your iOS is older than 16. Check in Settings > General > About.

A simple fix that often works: plug the iPhone in overnight with the screen locked, then look again in the morning. Source: Apple Support: If you can't find the Duplicates album.

Before you merge: iCloud Photos and Shared Library

  • If you use iCloud Photos, deleting or merging on your iPhone also applies on your iPad, Mac and other devices signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • In an iCloud Shared Photo Library, anyone in the library can delete items, and a photo someone else deletes disappears for you too.
  • Photos that were synced to the iPhone from a computer cannot be deleted in the Photos app; the Trash button is grey. Stop syncing them from the computer instead.

Empty Recently Deleted to get the space back

Merged copies sit in Recently Deleted for 30 days and still use storage until then. To free the space now:

  1. In Photos, tap Collections (or Albums), scroll down and tap Recently Deleted under Utilities.
  2. Tap View Album and unlock it with Face ID or Touch ID.
  3. Tap Select, check nothing you need is there, then tap the More button and Delete All.
  4. Confirm. Items deleted from this album cannot be recovered.

Changed your mind? In the same album, tap Select, choose the photo, tap More, then Recover. To see how much space you have, go to Settings > General > iPhone Storage.

Tips for a cleaner library

  • Photos saved from WhatsApp and other chat apps are a common source of copies. Turning off automatic saving of chat media stops new ones arriving.
  • Check the Duplicates album once a month. It refills slowly as new copies are found.
  • Merging handles exact copies. Bursts and look-alike shots need a human eye: pick the sharpest one and delete the rest. Our guide on how to delete similar photos and keep the best one explains a quick way to choose.
