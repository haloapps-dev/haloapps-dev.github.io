How to find large videos taking up space on Android

When your phone says storage is full, a handful of long videos are often the reason. A few minutes of 4K video can take more space than hundreds of photos. The trick is to sort by size, so the biggest files come first, and then decide one by one. Here is where to find your largest videos on Android, phone by phone.

Find large videos with Files by Google

Files by Google is on most Android phones (it may be called just Files). It has two ways in.

Option 1: the "Delete large files" card

  1. Open Files by Google.
  2. Tap Menu, then Clean.
  3. On the Delete large files card, tap Select files.
  4. Check each file before you select it. Tap a video to play it if you are not sure what it is.
  5. At the bottom, tap Move x file(s) to Trash, then confirm.

Option 2: sort your videos by size

  1. In Files by Google, under Categories, tap Videos.
  2. At the top right, tap More, then Sort by, then Size.
  3. The largest videos are now at the top. Delete the ones you don't need.

The card does the searching for you; sorting shows every video, so you can also spot big screen recordings and downloaded clips. Deleted files go to the Files Trash first, where they stay for 30 days.

Use Settings > Storage

  1. Open your phone's Settings app and tap Storage.
  2. Tap a category to see what is using the space. Videos are often listed on their own, or inside Images or Media.
  3. To pick from a list, tap Free up space, scroll to the category you want and tap Select files. If the category isn't listed, tap Browse.
  4. Select the files and tap Move files to Trash.

Google says some of these steps only work on Android 14 and up, and phone makers change this screen, so yours may look different.

On a Samsung Galaxy: My Files

  1. Open My Files (in the Samsung folder on your apps screen, or search for it).
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Analyse storage. You can also tap More options (three dots), then Analyse storage.
  3. You'll see a breakdown of your storage. Scroll down to find duplicate, large and unused files.
  4. Open the large files list, select the videos you don't need and delete them. They go to the My Files Recycle bin for 30 days.

Exact names depend on your One UI version: Samsung UK says Analyse storage, Samsung US says Analyze storage.

Large videos in Google Photos (cloud storage)

  1. Open Google Photos and tap your profile picture or initial at the top.
  2. Tap Photos settings, then Backup, then Manage storage.
  3. Under the storage summary, open the category for large photos and videos and tap Select.
  4. Select the items and tap Move to trash.

Be careful here: this tool manages your Google Account storage. If a video is backed up, moving it to the Google Photos Trash removes it from your phone, your backup and every device signed in to that account. Where big videos hide

  • Screen recordings. Often kept in a separate Screen recordings or Movies folder, and easy to forget.
  • Chat apps. Videos you receive in WhatsApp and other messengers are saved separately from your camera. See how to clear WhatsApp media taking up space.
  • Downloads. Clips saved from the web or email land in the Downloads folder.
  • Copies. Edited or trimmed videos are saved as new files next to the original. See how to delete duplicate photos and videos on Android.

Before you delete a video

  • Play it first. Thumbnails of long videos all look alike. A few seconds tells you what it is.
  • Keep a copy of the ones that matter. If a video is precious, make sure it is backed up or copied to a computer before you remove it from the phone.
  • Empty the bin when you are sure. Deleted videos still use space while they sit in a Trash or Recycle bin. If you change your mind, see how to recover deleted photos and videos from the bin.

Common questions

Why do my videos take up so much space?

Video files grow with length, resolution and frame rate. A long video in 4K or at 60 frames per second can be very large. If you don't need that quality, check your camera app's video settings for a lower resolution.

Can I sort videos by size in the gallery app?

Many gallery apps sort by date only. File manager apps, such as Files by Google and Samsung My Files, let you sort by size, so use them to find the biggest files.

Will deleting a video from my phone delete it from Google Photos?

If you delete it in Google Photos and it is backed up, yes: it goes from the phone and the backup. Deleting a file in a file manager removes the copy on the phone; check Google Photos to see whether a backup copy is still there.
