How to delete screenshots on Android in bulk

Screenshots are taken in a second and kept for years: tickets, receipts, chats, maps, memes. Most were only useful for a day. Android keeps them together in one Screenshots folder, which makes them one of the easiest things to clear in bulk. Here are three ways, and how to save the few that matter.

Steps: delete old screenshots with Files by Google

  1. Open Files by Google.
  2. Tap Menu, then Clean.
  3. On the Old screenshots card, tap Select files. The card lists screenshots created or received more than 4 weeks ago.
  4. Select the screenshots you want to delete. Untick any you still need.
  5. Tap Move to Trash at the bottom, then confirm.

If the card is not there, Files has not found screenshots older than 4 weeks. Source: Files by Google Help: Delete your screenshots.

Steps: delete the Screenshots album in Samsung Gallery

  1. Open Gallery and tap Albums.
  2. Open the Screenshots album.
  3. Touch and hold one thumbnail to start selecting. To take the whole album, tap All at the top; then untick the ones you want to keep.
  4. Tap Delete, then Move to Trash to confirm.

The steps follow Samsung: Use the Gallery app. The All option and its position can differ between One UI versions.

Steps: delete screenshots in Google Photos

  1. Open Google Photos and tap Collections at the bottom (called Library in older versions).
  2. Open the Screenshots folder in the list of folders on your device.
  3. Tap and hold a screenshot, then tap more of them to select several. You can usually drag your finger across thumbnails to select a run of them.
  4. Tap Trash at the bottom.

Before you do this, know one thing: deleting in Google Photos removes the screenshot from your phone and from your Google Photos backup on every device where backup is on. If you only want to clear the phone copy, select the items and use More > Delete from device. Deleted items stay in Trash for 30 days. Source: Google Photos Help: Delete photos & videos.

Before you clear them all: save what matters

  • Look for tickets, booking codes, receipts, warranty details and addresses. Move those to a separate album, or write the detail down, before you delete the rest.
  • Screenshots of chats or photos someone sent you may be the only copy you have. Keep the ones you care about.
  • Screenshots of things you meant to read or buy are rarely needed later. If you have not opened it in a month, you probably will not.

Empty the trash to get the space back

Files, Samsung Gallery and Google Photos all move deleted items to a trash first, and they still use space until it is emptied. In Samsung Gallery: Menu > Trash > More options > Empty. In Google Photos: Collections > Trash > More > Empty Trash. Check nothing you need is there first, because emptied items cannot be restored.

Stop them piling up again

  • Spend a couple of minutes each week clearing new screenshots, instead of an hour once a year.
  • When you take a screenshot for a one-off task, delete it as soon as the task is done.
