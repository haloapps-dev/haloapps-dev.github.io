Coral Pour privacy policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the game Coral Pour: Reef Color Sort ("Coral Pour", "the game") uses, why, and the choices you have. Coral Pour is published by Halo Apps ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email haloapps.dev@gmail.com.

In short
  • Your levels, shells, rewards and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • There is no account, and we do not run servers that receive your data. We do not sell your data.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, personalised ads are only shown with your consent, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • If the game crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (by Google) sends us a technical crash report.
  • Coral Pour sends usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), such as levels played, so we can make the game better. Where the law requires it, this only happens with your consent.
  • Purchases and the Reef Club subscription are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

Coral Pour stores your game progress, rewards, collected items, settings, purchase status and ad consent choices only on your device, in the game's private storage. We cannot access this information. You can delete it at any time by clearing the game's data in your phone's settings or uninstalling the game.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Coral Pour shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google. Fewer or no ads are shown if you have bought an ad-free pack or joined the Reef Club. Watching a rewarded ad is always optional.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location (country or city level);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, screen size and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information about the ads SDK.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, Coral Pour uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are requested. If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use data such as your approximate location and device information for purposes like frequency capping, fraud prevention and aggregated reporting. Where US state privacy laws apply, a similar message lets you opt out of the use of your data for targeted advertising.

Changing your choices.

  • In Coral Pour: Settings › Privacy choices (shown where your region requires it).
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Google: My Ad Center.

Google acts as an independent controller for some of this data. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, How Google uses cookies and other identifiers in advertising and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To find and fix bugs, Coral Pour uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google. If the game crashes or hits a serious error, Crashlytics sends a report containing the technical details of the error (stack trace), device model, operating system version, game version, the time of the crash and a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics. Crashlytics also records when the game is opened (an anonymous session record), so we can see what share of sessions are crash-free. We use crash reports only to make the game more reliable. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and where it can be improved, Coral Pour uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. It records game events such as starting, winning or failing a level, using power-ups, completing tasks, opening the shop and making purchases, and the estimated revenue of ads shown in the game. Analytics also records standard app events (first open, sessions, app updates), the device model, operating system, game version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address (country or region) and a random app instance identifier created by Analytics.

Where GDPR applies, Analytics is only switched on if you agreed to storing information on your device and to measurement in the consent message, and withdrawing consent in Privacy choices turns it off. Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months. See Google Analytics data practices and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Reminders and notifications

After you have played a few levels, Coral Pour asks once whether you would like reminders. If you agree and allow notifications, the game schedules a few reminders on your phone (for example a day, three days and a week after you last played); opening the game cancels them, and they stop after a week. These reminders are created on your device and send nothing to us. You can turn them off at any time in the game's Settings or in your phone's notification settings.

While reminders are on, Coral Pour also registers with Firebase Cloud Messaging, a service of Google, so we can occasionally send a notification about something new in the game (such as new levels) to players in a given language or country. Firebase Cloud Messaging uses a random registration token for your installation and does not receive your name or contact details. Where GDPR applies, registration only starts after you have answered the consent message. Turning reminders off stops it. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Optional in-game purchases, such as shell packs and ad-free packs, and the Reef Club subscription are processed by Google Play Billing under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The game only receives confirmation from Google Play that the purchase was made and saves that on your device. You can manage or cancel the Reef Club subscription at any time in the Google Play app under Payments & subscriptions.

7. Ratings

Coral Pour may occasionally offer to let you rate the game. This uses the review screen built into Google Play; any review you write is handled by Google and is not seen by us inside the game.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer an account.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not collect your contacts, messages, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not share data with anyone other than the service providers described above.

9. Legal bases (UK and EU GDPR)

Halo Apps is the controller for the processing described in this policy, Google is an independent controller for some advertising data and Google is our processor for Firebase. We rely on:

  • consent for personalised advertising, usage statistics (Analytics) and reminders and notifications (your choice in the game and your phone's notification permission);
  • legitimate interests for showing non-personalised ads that fund the free game, preventing ad fraud, and crash reporting to keep the game working;
  • performance of a contract for providing purchases and subscriptions you buy.

Google may process data in countries outside the UK and the EEA, including the United States, using safeguards recognised by law such as standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because Coral Pour has no account and we do not hold data linked to your identity, most of these rights are easiest to exercise with the controls above (in-game privacy choices, your phone's ad settings, uninstalling the game) or directly with Google. You can also email us and we will help.

If you are in the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In the EEA you can complain to your local data protection authority.

US residents. We do not sell personal information for money. Use of advertising identifiers by the ads SDK may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising under some US state laws; you can opt out using the in-game privacy choices (where shown) or your device's ad settings.

11. Children

Coral Pour is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the game and you have concerns, contact us and we will help.

12. Security

Data stored by Coral Pour stays in the game's private storage on your device. Data sent by the Google services described above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

13. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example when the game adds a new feature or service, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change materially affects how your data is used, we will also tell you in the game where appropriate.
