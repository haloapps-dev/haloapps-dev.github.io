Setup or Skip privacy policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information the app Setup or Skip: Chart Patterns ("Setup or Skip", "the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. Setup or Skip is published by Halo Apps ("we", "us"). If you have any questions, email haloapps.dev@gmail.com.

In short
  • Your lessons, answers, XP, streak, equity curve and settings are stored only on your phone.
  • There is no account, and we do not run servers that receive your data. We do not sell your data.
  • The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. Google collects some device data to show and measure ads. Where the law requires it, personalised ads are only shown with your consent, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • If the app crashes, Firebase Crashlytics (by Google) sends us a technical crash report.
  • The app sends usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase), such as which lessons and sessions are played, so we can make it better. Where the law requires it, this only happens with your consent, and you can switch it off in Settings.
  • Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Information stored on your device

Setup or Skip stores your progress (lessons read, answers, XP, level, streak, mistakes to review and your equity curve), your settings, your purchase status and your ad consent choices only on your device, in the app's private storage. We cannot access this information. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your phone's settings or uninstalling the app.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free plan shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google: optional rewarded videos (for an extra session or to repair a streak) and, at most once every few sessions, a full-screen ad when you leave a session summary. There are no ads between cards. Pro removes the full-screen ads.

To show ads, limit how often you see them, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app collects and shares with Google:

  • device identifiers, such as the advertising ID and the app set ID;
  • your IP address, which can be used to estimate your approximate location (country or city level);
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system, language, screen size and app version;
  • interactions with ads, such as ad views and taps, and diagnostic information about the ads SDK.

Consent. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent before ads are requested. If you do not consent to personalised ads, Google may still show non-personalised ads, which use data such as your approximate location and device information for purposes like frequency capping, fraud prevention and aggregated reporting. Where US state privacy laws apply, a similar message lets you opt out of the use of your data for targeted advertising.

Changing your choices.

  • In the app: Settings › Ad privacy choices (shown where your region requires it).
  • Android: reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (usually Settings › Privacy › Ads, or Settings › Google › Ads).
  • Google: My Ad Center.

Google acts as an independent controller for some of this data. Learn more in the Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services, How Google uses cookies and other identifiers in advertising and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure.

3. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

To find and fix bugs, the app uses Firebase Crashlytics, a service of Google. If the app crashes or hits a serious error, Crashlytics sends a report containing the technical details of the error (stack trace), device model, operating system version, app version, the time of the crash and a random installation identifier created by Crashlytics. Crashlytics also records when the app is opened (an anonymous session record), so we can see what share of sessions are crash-free. We use crash reports only to make the app more reliable. Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4. Usage statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To understand how the app is used and where it can be improved, the app uses Google Analytics for Firebase, a service of Google. It records events such as finishing onboarding or a lesson, starting and finishing a session, each answer (the mode, the pattern, the practice card's id, your swipe, whether it was right and the R it scored), sharing the daily result, reporting a card, opening the Pro screen, buying or restoring Pro (the product only) and the estimated revenue of ads shown in the app. Analytics also records standard app events (first open, sessions, app updates, in-app purchases), the device model, operating system, app version, language, approximate location derived from the IP address (country or region) and a random app instance identifier created by Analytics. It never uses the advertising ID or Android ID, and the events contain nothing about who you are.

Where GDPR applies, Analytics is only switched on if you agreed to storing information on your device and to measurement in the consent message. Everywhere, you can switch it off in Settings › Usage statistics. Analytics data is kept for at most 14 months. See Google Analytics data practices and Privacy and Security in Firebase.

5. Purchases, subscriptions and settings from Remote Config

Pro (monthly, yearly with a free trial, or lifetime) is sold through Google Play Billing under Google's own terms and privacy policy. We never receive your name, email address or payment details. The app only receives confirmation from Google Play that a purchase or subscription is active and saves that on your device. You manage or cancel subscriptions in Google Play.

The app also downloads settings such as ad timing and the number of free sessions from Firebase Remote Config, a service of Google. This uses the same random app instance identifier as Analytics and no personal information.

6. What we do not do

  • We do not require or offer an account.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not offer trading, investing or any financial service, and we do not pass your data to brokers.
  • We do not collect your contacts, messages, photos, files, precise location, microphone or camera data.
  • We do not share data with anyone other than the service providers described above.

7. Legal bases (UK and EU GDPR)

Halo Apps is the controller for the processing described in this policy, Google is an independent controller for some advertising data and Google is our processor for Firebase. We rely on:

  • consent for personalised advertising and usage statistics (Analytics);
  • legitimate interests for showing non-personalised ads that fund the free plan, preventing ad fraud, and crash reporting to keep the app working;
  • performance of a contract for providing Pro when you buy it.

Google may process data in countries outside the UK and the EEA, including the United States, using safeguards recognised by law such as standard contractual clauses and the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and its UK extension.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app has no account and we do not hold data linked to your identity, most of these rights are easiest to exercise with the controls above (the app's privacy choices and Usage statistics switch, your phone's ad settings, uninstalling the app) or directly with Google. You can also email us and we will help.

If you are in the UK you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk). In the EEA you can complain to your local data protection authority.

US residents. We do not sell personal information for money. Use of advertising identifiers by the ads SDK may count as "sharing" for targeted advertising under some US state laws; you can opt out using the app's privacy choices (where shown) or your device's ad settings.

9. Children

Setup or Skip is meant for adults (18 and over) and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the app and you have concerns, contact us and we will help.

10. Security

Data stored by the app stays in its private storage on your device. Data sent by the Google services described above is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, for example when the app adds a new feature or service, we will update this page and the effective date above. If a change materially affects how your data is used, we will also tell you in the app where appropriate.
