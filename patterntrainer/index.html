Setup or Skip: Chart Patterns

Learn to read candlestick charts one swipe at a time. Short lessons first, then spot the pattern, then judge the setup: take it or skip it, and see what happened next, scored in R.

  • Lessons before tests. Eight patterns, from hammers to double tops, each with a checklist and annotated examples.
  • Judge setups, not luck. Decisions are graded apart from results: a good setup that lost is still a good call.
  • Fair. Every pattern is free to learn, there are no ads between cards, and it works offline with no account.
  • Education only. No real trading, no money to win or withdraw, no signals and no brokers.
